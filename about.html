WHO WE ARE

Building Connections. Creating Possibilities.

Highpoint Services Network Philippines, Inc. (HSN Philippines) is a comprehensive Information and Communications Technology (ICT) services and business solutions provider.

As a subsidiary of Acer Inc., HSN Philippines supports businesses through business process outsourcing, contact center operations, omnichannel customer experience solutions, and a full range of ICT services.

The company also provides after-sales and repair services, spare parts distribution, warehousing, and logistics solutions for major technology brands across the Asia-Pacific region.
