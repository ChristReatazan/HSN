What We Collect

The personal information we collect depends on your relationship and transactions with HSNP.

A. Prospective and Existing Customers

This includes customers with terminated services. We may collect the following information:

  1. Information you provide when availing of our services, such as your name, address, phone number, email address, Tax Identification Number (TIN), proof of authority to occupy premises (such as a lease contract, Transfer Certificate of Title, Special Power of Attorney, or authorization from the property owner), and, where applicable, details of your authorized representative.
  2. Billing and payment information used to settle your service transactions, including check and card payments. Payment instrument details are captured and processed by the applicable payment gateway service provider.
  3. Information needed to determine eligibility for promotional activities, contact center or hotline services, and customer information sheets.
  4. Information you submit through forms on our website, including inquiries, complaints, customer portal registration, online applications, billing, and payment services.
  5. Information you provide when communicating with HSNP or our representatives, including inquiries and complaint details relating to the quality and reliability of services.
  6. Responses you or your representative provide when participating in customer surveys.
  7. Information provided for verification purposes, such as to facilitate refunds or zero-rated VAT transactions, including a copy of a valid government-issued identification card or foreign passport.
  8. Any other information you voluntarily provide for a legitimate purpose disclosed at the point of collection.

B. Prospective, Active, and Separated Employees

  1. Information submitted when applying for work at HSNP, including information in your resume, curriculum vitae, application form, and work references.
  2. Information collected during application processing, such as testing results, employment offers, character investigation results, and pre-employment medical assessment information.
  3. Information collected and maintained during employment, including payroll information, government-mandated and third-party remittances such as SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG contributions, taxes, bank account information, wages, entitlements, benefits, health and welfare benefits, medical and dental care records, beneficiary and emergency contact information, training and certifications, performance evaluations, sanctions, employment changes, and work history.
  4. Information retained after separation from service, such as beneficiary details and contact information.
  5. Any other information you voluntarily provide for a legitimate purpose disclosed at the point of collection.

C. Vendors, Suppliers, and Contractors

  1. Information submitted in accreditation applications, supply chain application systems, and payment processing, such as your name, Tax Identification Number, address, contact details, educational attainment, work experience, and banking information.
  2. Any other information you voluntarily provide for a legitimate purpose disclosed at the point of collection.

D. Stakeholders and Shareholders

  1. Information submitted when becoming a shareholder or during the course of being a shareholder of HSNP, such as your name, address, contact details, marital status, government-issued identification, and, where applicable, details of your authorized representative and heirs.
  2. Any other information you voluntarily provide for a legitimate purpose disclosed at the point of collection.

E. Guests and Visitors

  1. Information provided when entering our premises, such as your name, address, vehicle type, plate number, or conduction sticker number.
  2. Any other information you voluntarily provide for a legitimate purpose disclosed at the point of collection.
02

What We Do With the Information We Gather

HSNP stores, processes, and analyzes personal information for legitimate and declared business purposes, including the following:

A. Prospective and Existing Customers

  1. Providing and continuously improving our services and managing your account.
  2. Responding to your inquiries, concerns, or complaints.
  3. Sending information about programs and service offerings that may be of interest to you.
  4. Sending service-related messages, updates, alerts, and other information you request.
  5. Enabling us to accept payments according to your enrolled payment method.
  6. Verifying your identity when accessing your account through email, website, mobile application, telephone, or in-person channels.
  7. Verifying your identity and eligibility for warranty claims, where applicable.

B. Prospective, Active, and Separated Employees

  1. Evaluating eligibility for employment, including verification of qualifications and character references through background checks.
  2. Administering pay, statutory deductions, entitlements, and benefits.
  3. Complying with applicable statutory and regulatory requirements and submissions.
  4. Conducting performance reviews and administering rewards.
  5. Establishing appropriate training and development interventions.
  6. Administering disciplinary actions and sanctions.
  7. Collecting and maintaining contact information.
  8. Maintaining employment records.
  9. Processing work-related employee claims, such as workers' compensation and insurance claims.
  10. Developing health and wellness programs.

C. Vendors, Suppliers, and Contractors

  1. Verifying and confirming your identity as part of vendor accreditation.
  2. Conducting business with you.
  3. Establishing and managing business relationships.
  4. Facilitating payment of invoices for goods delivered or services rendered.
  5. Complying with statutory, legal, and regulatory requirements.
  6. Updating and maintaining vendor account information.
  7. Maintaining details of authorized contact persons for supplied goods or services.
  8. Responding to questions, comments, and feedback, and communicating requirements, programs, or advisories through appropriate channels. Information may also be used for internal administrative purposes, such as auditing, data analysis, and database records management.

D. Shareholders

  1. Maintaining our shareholder register.
  2. Managing your account and our relationship with you.
  3. Handling dividend and other payments.
  4. Communicating with you, responding to queries and requests, and sending notices of general meetings, annual reports, and shareholder circulars.
  5. Registering shareholders at general meetings and shareholder events, including verification of your identity or proxy.
  6. Addressing legal and investor relations matters.

E. Visitors and Guests

  1. Establishing your identity.
  2. Recording the purpose of your visit.
  3. Monitoring visitor activities within company premises, including the entry and exit of equipment and vehicles.

Data Disclosure and Sharing

Access to your personal information is restricted to HSNP employees and contractors who need the information to perform their responsibilities in conducting our business.

Where contractors are authorized to access personal information, HSNP requires them through a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) to protect and maintain the confidentiality of such information. Contractors are not permitted to disclose your information to unauthorized parties or use it for their own purposes.

Your information may also be disclosed to government entities when required or permitted under applicable laws and regulations, or pursuant to a valid subpoena or court order.

04

Information Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. HSNP undertakes and implements reasonable organizational, physical, and technical security measures when collecting, receiving, transmitting, storing, and disposing of personal information.

For an enhanced online experience, our services may be accessed through compatible devices such as laptops, personal computers, tablets, and mobile phones. For added security, we recommend installing and maintaining reputable antivirus software on devices used to access the internet.

You are responsible for protecting your information once it reaches you or your authorized representative through any medium, including written correspondence, bills, emails, system applications, and online accounts. Please take appropriate measures to ensure that any medium or device used to access or manage your information is secure and not accessible to unauthorized persons.

05

Automatic Collection of Information and Cookies

When you use our website or services available through it, cookies and similar technologies may be used through our website and, where applicable, our email and SMS communications. You may choose to accept or decline cookies through your browser settings.

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies. However, you can modify your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality and your browsing experience.

A cookie is a small file that requests permission to be placed on your device. Once accepted, the file is added and helps analyze web traffic or recognize visits to a particular website. Cookies allow web applications to respond to you as an individual by remembering certain preferences.

We may use traffic log cookies to identify which pages are being used. This helps us analyze website traffic and improve the browsing experience. A cookie does not give us access to your computer or personal information beyond the data you choose to share with us.

When you interact with our website, mobile website, emails, and online advertising, we may automatically collect and temporarily store certain technical information, such as the time spent on the site, page views, your device's IP address, and the browser and operating system being used. This information helps monitor functionality, identify areas for improvement, and support website operations. Such information is stored in log files and removed in accordance with applicable retention practices.

We encourage you to exercise caution and review the privacy policy of every external website you visit.

08

Personal Data Retention and Disposal

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary for the following purposes:

  1. To fulfill the declared, specified, and legitimate purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or until processing relevant to those purposes has been completed or terminated.
  2. To establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  3. To fulfill other legitimate business purposes consistent with standards established or approved by regulatory agencies governing HSNP.

After the applicable retention period, your personal data will be securely disposed of or discarded in a manner intended to prevent further processing, unauthorized access, or disclosure to any other party or the public.
